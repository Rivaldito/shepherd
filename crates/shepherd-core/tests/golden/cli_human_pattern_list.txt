NAME	KIND	SOURCE	SHADOWED	AGENTS	TOPICS	DESCRIPTION
agentcoder	blackboard	builtin	no	3	1	Programmer, test designer and executor, after the AgentCoder paper. Starts: programmer (posts `code:` to `board`). The test designer posts independent tests (basic, edge and large cases) written from the spec only; the executor runs them and sends the real errors to the programmer. Capped at 4 rounds.
blackboard	blackboard	builtin	no	3	1	Shared knowledge base where specialist agents read hypotheses and post discoveries. Starts: researcher (posts the first hypothesis to `blackboard`). Analyst and synthesizer wait for entries on `blackboard`, then post validations and the final synthesis there.
hierarchical	hierarchical	builtin	no	7	4	Multi-tier command hierarchy where managers supervise specialized worker teams. Starts: director (level 0; publishes directives to `root-orders`). Managers wait for `root-orders`, publish assignments to `team-orders` and report to `root-results`; specialists wait for `team-orders` and publish to `team-results`.
market	market	builtin	no	6	3	Competitive market where solvers bid on asks coordinated by a market-maker. Starts: requesters (publish `ask` from the objective). Solvers wait for `ask` and publish `bid`; the market-maker waits for `ask`/`bid` and publishes the `transaction`; requesters and solvers then react to it.
mutation-testing	blackboard	builtin	no	3	1	Measures whether the tests are worth anything. Starts: mutator (posts `mutants:` to `board`: deliberate bugs in the code, each on its own branch). The test writer adds tests that must fail on every mutant and pass on the original; the judge runs them and posts the score (killed / total) and the survivors. Capped at 3 rounds.
orchestrator-worker	orchestrator-worker	builtin	no	4	2	Central orchestrator dispatches partitioned tasks to a worker pool and aggregates results. Starts: orchestrator (receives the objective, publishes task orders to `orders`). Workers wait for `orders` and publish results to `results`; the orchestrator aggregates them.
planner-workers-reviewer	orchestrator-worker	builtin	no	5	3	Planner, workers and a reviewer, for tests across modules. Starts: planner (splits the objective into modules and publishes one order per module to `orders`). Each worker writes the unit tests of its module and publishes the result to `results`; the reviewer checks coverage and quality of every result and publishes `reviews`; the planner aggregates only what the reviewer approved. Nothing is merged by the run.
reflection-loop	blackboard	builtin	no	2	1	Generator and critic: one agent writes code or tests, the other reviews them on a different vendor and sends comments back until it approves. Starts: generator (posts `draft:` to `board`). The critic answers `critique:` (REQUEST_CHANGES with file:line comments, or APPROVE). Capped at 3 rounds; after that both stop and the run shows as stalled for a human.
self-debug	blackboard	builtin	no	2	1	Reflexion and self-debug. Starts: solver (posts `attempt:` to `board`). When the real tests fail, the solver reads the traceback and posts a `reflection:` (what failed, why, what to try); the debugger reproduces the failure independently and posts a `diagnosis:`. Every next attempt starts by reading all earlier reflections on the board, which is the memory. Capped at 4 attempts.
tdd	blackboard	builtin	no	3	1	Multi-agent TDD. Starts: test-writer (posts `tests:` to `board`, written from the spec alone, so it never tailors them to an implementation). The coder implements until they pass; the runner runs the real test command and posts the exact failures back. Capped at 4 rounds, then it stops for a human.
